### About

2027), completing a **Master's in Data Science** at the University of Carthage at the
same time — and working full-time as an **AI & Backend Developer**.

I chose the second degree, and I fund it myself. I didn't want to pick between building

going back to the statistics underneath them.

**I'm looking for a research internship in AI/ML** — especially where language models,

### Featured work

**🌡️ [PCA of Tunisian Temperatures](https://github.com/EyaAzzabi/pca-tunisia-temperatures)**
Given only twelve monthly averages per station and no geography whatsoever, PCA
reconstructed the two physical axes governing Tunisian climate — explaining **98.4%** of
variation across 16 stations. Component 1 turned out to be mean annual temperature
(r = 0.9996); component 2, the Mediterranean's moderating effect (r = 0.9893).
Extends my bachelor's thesis, supervised by Prof. Chaouki Aouiti.
`Python` `NumPy` `PCA`

**🦯 SenseMate** — IoT assistive technology for blind and visually impaired people.
Real-time object detection and identification with YOLO, built with **Enactus FSB** over
15 months as a social-impact project.
`PyTorch` `YOLO` `IoT` `Computer Vision`

**🩺 [PCOS Prediction](https://github.com/EyaAzzabi/Pcos_Prediction)**
Early detection of polycystic ovary syndrome from clinical and hormonal features.
Six models compared (Logistic Regression → XGBoost), SMOTE for class imbalance,
evaluated on ROC/AUC and F1. XGBoost came out ahead.
`scikit-learn` `XGBoost` `SMOTE` `Healthcare ML`

**📧 Email Fraud Detection** — binary classification with SVM and Random Forest over
TF-IDF features. **94% precision.**
`scikit-learn` `NLP` `TF-IDF`

**🤖 Smart Campus AI Assistant** — hackathon project: a locally-run chatbot plus a
lost-and-found assistant using CLIP for image retrieval.
`NLP` `CLIP` `LLM`
